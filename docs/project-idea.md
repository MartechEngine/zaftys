## One-line

**ZAFTYS is India's industrial logistics ecosystem**  -  company-operated transport, the ZAFTYS TSM platform, and the TranZfort verified capacity network under one registered company.

---

## The problem

Industrial shippers and transporters manage freight through fragmented providers, phone-based coordination, limited visibility, and capacity that does not scale cleanly when demand spikes. Technology is often bolted on instead of built around how heavy freight actually runs.

---

## The ZAFTYS answer

Three capabilities, one operating model:

| Layer | What it is | Who it serves |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| **ZAFTYS Logistics** | Own fleet + professional dispatch on industrial corridors | Manufacturers, suppliers, enterprises needing dependable FTL and contract programs |
| **ZAFTYS TSM** | Transport & fleet management platform (dispatch, GPS, ePOD, fleet records, client portal) | Used internally daily; offered to shippers and fleet operators who want the same operational discipline |
| **TranZfort** | Verified partner network for overflow capacity | Customers when own fleet is full; fleet owners seeking structured industrial loads through ZAFTYS |

**Critical rule:** Every commercial transaction runs through **ZAFTYS Logistics Pvt Ltd**. TranZfort is capacity and coordination  -  not a separate vendor relationship for ZAFTYS clients.

---

## Vision

To become India's most trusted **industrial logistics ecosystem** by combining operational excellence, intelligent technology, and scalable transport capacity into one unified platform  -  where every shipment is visible, every partner is accountable, and logistics decisions are supported by accurate information.

---

## Mission

Simplify industrial logistics by helping businesses move freight more efficiently through integrated transport operations, intelligent software, and trusted transport partnerships  -  measured by customer confidence in every shipment, not load count alone.

---

## Primary audiences

1. **Industrial shippers & manufacturers**  -  cement, steel, mining, chemicals, FMCG, multi-plant supply chains  
2. **Large transporters & enterprises**  -  recurring corridors, SLAs, visibility requirements  
3. **Fleet owners & transport partners**  -  verified loads, payments via ZAFTYS, professional onboarding through TranZfort
