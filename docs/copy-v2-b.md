# Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

---

### What industries does ZAFTYS support?

ZAFTYS provides logistics solutions for manufacturers, mining companies, cement producers, steel businesses, construction companies, engineering firms, distributors, and other industrial organizations requiring dependable freight transportation.

---

### Do I need to use ZAFTYS TSM to transport my freight?

No.

Customers can use ZAFTYS logistics services without purchasing the software platform.

ZAFTYS TSM enhances operational visibility and transport management, while logistics services remain available independently.

---

### Can ZAFTYS manage recurring transport requirements?

Yes.

We support both one time shipments and long term logistics partnerships designed around recurring transportation requirements.

---

### What happens when additional trucks are required?

When demand exceeds available fleet capacity, ZAFTYS expands transport resources through the verified TranZfort network while maintaining centralized operational coordination.

---

### Which cargo types do you transport?

Transport capabilities depend on operational requirements, cargo characteristics, vehicle availability, and applicable regulations.

Our logistics specialists evaluate each requirement individually before recommending the most suitable transport solution.

---

### How do I request a transport quotation?

Simply submit your transport requirement through our enquiry form or contact our logistics team.

After reviewing your shipment details, we will recommend an appropriate transport solution.

---

### Can I become a transport partner?

Yes.

Fleet owners and transport businesses can apply to join the TranZfort network through our partner registration process.

Every application is reviewed before onboarding.

### Do you provide shipment visibility?

Yes.
