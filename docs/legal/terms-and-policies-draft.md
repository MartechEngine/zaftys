## 2. How ZAFTYS operates

### 2.1 Two modes of business

| Mode | What happens | ZAFTYS role | Typical fee |
|------|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| **A — Owned fleet** | ZAFTYS uses trucks it owns/operates | May act as performing carrier for that trip (subject to trip documents) | Freight / carriage charges |
| **B — Marketplace / capacity facilitation** | ZAFTYS finds a third-party truck for a Transporter (e.g. CJ Darcl and others) | **Capacity facilitator / broker** — introduces capacity, shares details, earns facilitation fee | Broker / facilitation fee |

### 2.2 Marketplace matching flow (Mode B)

1. Transporter places an order / capacity request with ZAFTYS.
2. ZAFTYS checks its network for a suitable truck on that route.
3. ZAFTYS sends truck details and owner/operator details (and available documents) to the Transporter.
4. **Transporter reviews and approves** the proposed truck.
5. Only after approval does the truck proceed for loading.

**Legal framing:** In Mode B, ZAFTYS is a **capacity facilitator**, not the default performing carrier, and not a guarantor of the trucker’s performance, documents, or conduct — unless ZAFTYS expressly issues an LR / takes custody in writing for that trip.

```text
Transporter  -->  order  -->  ZAFTYS
ZAFTYS       -->  match + share truck/owner docs  -->  Transporter
Transporter  -->  verifies & approves  -->  ZAFTYS
ZAFTYS       -->  dispatch coordination  -->  Third-party trucker
Trucker      -->  performs carriage  -->  load / unload
```

## 5. Terms of Service — detailed clauses

### 5.1 Acceptance

- By accessing the website, registering as a partner, requesting capacity, approving a truck, or using ZAFTYS services, the party agrees to these Terms.
- Users must be 18+ and legally able to bind their organisation.
- If a company uses the services, the individual represents they are authorised to bind that company.

### 5.2 Nature of services — capacity facilitator

ZAFTYS provides:

1. Transportation and related logistics services using **owned fleet** where applicable  
2. **Capacity facilitation / marketplace matching** of third-party trucks to Transporters  
3. Coordination, communication, and (where applicable) GST-compliant commercial billing related to placements  
4. Informational website content and software products (e.g. TMS / TranZfort demos or tools)

**Critical statement for Mode B:**

> ZAFTYS acts as a capacity facilitator. Unless ZAFTYS expressly agrees in writing that it is the Performing Carrier for a Trip (including issuance of LR / taking custody of goods), ZAFTYS does not undertake carriage of goods by the third-party truck, does not control the trucker’s day-to-day operations, and is not responsible for the trucker’s performance, legality, or cargo outcomes.

### 5.3 Relationship of parties

- Transporter and Fleet Partner are independent parties.
- No employment, partnership, or agency is created that makes ZAFTYS liable for the Fleet Partner’s acts as employer or joint venture partner (except limited agency to facilitate introduction / messaging as described).
- Fleet Partners are independent contractors / independent carriers.

### 5.4 Matching and Approval workflow

1. Transporter submits requirements (route, vehicle type, timing, cargo class as disclosed).  
2. ZAFTYS searches its network and may propose one or more trucks.  
3. ZAFTYS shares available truck and owner details and Documents with the Transporter.  
4. Transporter must complete Verification (Section 5.5) before Approval.  
5. Upon Approval, ZAFTYS coordinates dispatch messaging as facilitator.  
6. The Performing Carrier proceeds for loading as instructed by the Transporter / loading point.

**No Approval = no dispatch obligation** for that proposed truck.

### 5.5 Transporter’s duty to verify (core protection)

**Position:** Transporters are responsible for verifying all truck and owner details and Documents that ZAFTYS provides before Approval and before allowing loading.

The Transporter must independently verify, to its own satisfaction:

- Vehicle identity and registration (RC)
- Fitness, permits, insurance, PUC (as applicable to the Trip)
- Driver identity and valid driving licence for the vehicle class
- Owner / operator identity and authority to deploy the vehicle
- Suitability of vehicle for cargo type, weight, dimensions, route, and timeline
- Any other compliance the Transporter or its shipper requires

**Legal effects:**

- ZAFTYS shares Documents **as received or as available** for facilitation only.
- Sharing is **not a warranty** of authenticity, completeness, accuracy, currency, or fitness for purpose.
- ZAFTYS does not guarantee that any Document is genuine or that the truck/driver is legally compliant.
- By Approving, the Transporter confirms it has conducted (or knowingly waived) Verification and accepts the truck for that Trip.
- ZAFTYS shall not be liable for loss arising from forged, expired, incomplete, or inaccurate Documents, or from the Transporter’s failure to verify.

### 5.6 Fleet Partner obligations

Fleet Partners agree to:

- Provide true, current Documents and information
- Maintain valid licences, permits, insurance, and vehicle fitness
- Perform the Trip with due care; not engage in theft, fraud, illegal carriage, or other unlawful acts
- Follow lawful instructions for pickup/delivery reporting
- Not hold ZAFTYS liable for Transporter payment disputes beyond ZAFTYS’s agreed facilitation role
- Accept Halting rules in Section 6 when Free Time is exceeded

### 5.7 Cargo, theft, delay, and illegal acts — liability allocation

#### Mode B (marketplace / facilitation)

ZAFTYS is **not liable** to the Transporter, Fleet Partner, shipper, consignee, or any third party for:

| Risk | Who bears it |
|------|----------------|
| Cargo loss, damage, shortage, contamination | Performing Carrier (Fleet Partner), subject to their contract with Transporter / insurance |
| Theft, pilferage, hijacking | Performing Carrier / parties in custody chain — not ZAFTYS as facilitator |
| Late delivery, missed slots, transit delay | Performing Carrier and/or party causing delay (plant, consignor, consignee) |
| Illegal acts, fraud, smuggling, document forgery by trucker/driver/owner | Fleet Partner and responsible individuals |
| Disputes between Transporter and Fleet Partner | Those two parties directly |

**Indemnity:** Transporter and Fleet Partner each indemnify and hold harmless ZAFTYS from claims, losses, penalties, and costs arising from the above, and from their breach of these Terms, except to the extent caused by ZAFTYS’s proven wilful misconduct.

#### Mode A (owned fleet)

Where ZAFTYS is the Performing Carrier under written trip terms, carriage liability follows those trip terms / applicable law — still subject to overall liability caps and exclusions for consequential loss where enforceable.

### 5.8 Limitation of liability (general)

To the fullest extent permitted by law:

- ZAFTYS is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, punitive damages, or lost profits/revenues.
- For Mode B facilitation claims, any aggregate liability of ZAFTYS related to a Trip shall not exceed the **Facilitation Fee actually paid to ZAFTYS for that Trip**.

## 6. Halting / detention policy (brainstorm + chosen rules)

### 6.1 Why this matters

Trucks often wait at plants / warehouses. Without clear Free Time and rates:

- Truckers claim unpaid detention from ZAFTYS
- Transporters dispute “market rate”
- ZAFTYS gets stuck in the middle despite being only a facilitator

### 6.2 Industry context (brainstorm)

| Approach | Typical market use | Pros | Cons |
|----------|-------------------|------|------|
| **2–4 hours free per stop**, then hourly (₹200–500+/hr by vehicle) | Common spot / app-broker practice | Fair to truckers; sharp incentive to unload fast | Harsh for industrial plants; hard for CJ Darcl–style plant gates |
| **Escalating daily slabs** (e.g. ₹1,500 → ₹3,000/day) | Some organised platforms | Predictable daily billing | Needs published slab table by vehicle class |
| **% of freight per day** (e.g. 1/30 of trip freight) | Negotiation fallback | Scales with vehicle economics | Can be disputed if freight itself is disputed |
| **48 hours free, then market / fare-linked** | Your preferred commercial stance | Friendly to large Transporters; simple story | Must define clock start/stop and who pays |

### 6.3 Chosen policy for ZAFTYS Terms

**Free Time**

- **48 consecutive hours** of waiting are free of Halting charges **in aggregate** for that Trip (loading + unloading wait combined).
- Clock starts at first **reported arrival / gate-in** at the first stop where waiting begins.
- Transit time between origin and destination does **not** count as Halting.

**After Free Time**

- Halting is payable **by the Transporter to the Fleet Partner (trucker)**.
- **ZAFTYS is not the payer or guarantor** of Halting unless ZAFTYS is itself the party causing the delay under Mode A owned-fleet terms.

**Rate waterfall (use first available)**

1. Rate **pre-agreed in writing** for that Trip / booking; else  
2. **Prevailing market standard** for that vehicle class and corridor; else  
3. Fallback: **1/30 (one-thirtieth) of the agreed trip freight per calendar day**, applied pro-rata for time beyond Free Time.

**Evidence**

- GPS logs, app timestamps, gate stamps, or written reporting (email/WhatsApp) of arrival and release.
- Without reasonable evidence, Halting claims may be rejected.

**Release**

- Clock stops when the truck is released and free to leave (loaded or empty, as applicable).

**ZAFTYS role**

- May relay Halting claims between parties as facilitator.
- Not liable if Transporter fails to pay trucker Halting.

### 6.4 Alternative if counsel / ops prefer clearer plant accounting

Swap aggregate 48h for:

- **24 hours Free Time at loading** + **24 hours Free Time at unloading** (still 48h total, but split per gate).

This is a one-line change; keep rate waterfall the same.

### 6.5 Sample clause language (for counsel polish)

> Free Time of forty-eight (48) consecutive hours in aggregate shall apply to each Trip for waiting at loading and unloading points combined. Waiting time beyond Free Time (“Halting”) shall be payable by the Transporter directly to the Fleet Partner at (a) the rate agreed for the Trip, or if none (b) the prevailing market standard for the vehicle class and corridor, or if neither can be established (c) one-thirtieth (1/30) of the agreed trip freight per calendar day, pro-rated. ZAFTYS acts only as capacity facilitator and shall have no liability for unpaid Halting between Transporter and Fleet Partner.

### 7.2 Data we collect

- Identity and contact: name, phone, email, company

- Documents and KYC shared for matching (RC, licence, etc.)

### 7.3 How we use data

- Provide logistics and capacity facilitation services  
- Match trucks to Transporter requests  
- Share necessary truck/owner details with Transporters for Verification and Approval  
- Share necessary load/route details with Fleet Partners for acceptance  
- Billing, GST compliance, dispute handling  
- Improve website / TMS / TranZfort tools  
- Legal compliance and rights protection  
- Marketing only where permitted (with unsubscribe)

### 7.4 Sharing (marketplace-critical)

We do **not** sell personal data.

We may share data with:

- **Transporters** — truck, driver, and owner details needed for Verification and dispatch  
- **Fleet Partners** — load and location details needed to perform or decide on a Trip  
- Service providers (hosting, email, analytics) under confidentiality  
- Authorities when required by law  
- Professional advisors (legal, accounting) under confidentiality  

**Transparency line:** Participating in matching means identity and Document-related data will be shared with the other side of a proposed Trip.

### 7.5 Retention

Retain for as long as needed for Trips, billing, legal claims, and statutory record-keeping (suggest: commercial records typically multi-year; counsel to set exact periods under tax and DPDP expectations).

### 7.6 Security

Appropriate technical and organisational measures; no absolute security guarantee over internet transmission.

Subject to applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act framework as applicable):

- Access / correction of personal data

## 10. Operational checklist

To make Terms actually stick in practice:

1. Send truck/owner pack to Transporter with a short line: *“Please verify documents before approval. Approval confirms acceptance under ZAFTYS Terms.”*  
2. Keep written Approval (email/WhatsApp) on file for every marketplace Trip.  
3. State whether Mode A or Mode B on the placement note.  
4. Note Free Time (48h aggregate) and Halting waterfall on booking confirmation.  
5. Later: add checkbox on Partner registration and Transporter onboarding accepting Terms.  
6. Do not market “verified truck” as a legal guarantee without aligning marketing copy to Verification duty.
