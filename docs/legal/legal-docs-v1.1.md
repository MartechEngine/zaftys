# 18. Waiting Time and Detention (Halting)

Waiting time at loading or unloading locations may attract detention / halting charges.

### 18.1 Default Free Time (unless Trip Documentation states otherwise)

Unless a Trip-specific document states different detention terms:

1. **Free Time** is **forty-eight (48) consecutive hours** of waiting **in aggregate** for that Trip (loading and unloading wait combined).
2. Free Time starts at the first reported **arrival / gate-in** (or other agreed reporting time) at the first stop where waiting begins.
3. Transit time between origin and destination does **not** count as waiting / Free Time.
4. The waiting clock stops when the Vehicle is released and free to leave the relevant location (loaded or empty, as applicable).

### 18.2 Who pays after Free Time

Detention / halting beyond Free Time is payable by the **Transporter to the Fleet Partner** (or to the Performing Carrier, as applicable).

ZAFTYS may coordinate communication or collection administration of detention claims. **ZAFTYS does not automatically assume personal liability** for unpaid detention merely because it facilitated the Trip, and is not a guarantor of detention amounts between Transporter and Fleet Partner.

### 18.3 Default rate waterfall (unless Trip Documentation states otherwise)

Detention / halting after Free Time is charged at:

1. the rate **pre-agreed in writing** for that Trip; or if none,
2. the **prevailing market standard** for that vehicle class and corridor; or if neither can reasonably be established,
3. **one-thirtieth (1/30) of the agreed trip freight per calendar day**, applied pro-rata for time beyond Free Time.

### 18.4 Evidence

Detention claims should be supported by commercially reasonable evidence such as GPS logs, app timestamps, gate stamps, photographs or written reporting of arrival and release. Claims without reasonable evidence may be rejected by the paying party.

### 18.5 Trip-specific override

Where a Trip-specific document provides different detention terms (including a split such as 24 hours at loading + 24 hours at unloading), those terms prevail over this Section 18 default.
