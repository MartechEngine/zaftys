# ZAFTYS Intelligence — editorial methodology

Institutional byline for deep-research blog pages: **ZAFTYS Intelligence**.

## What this means

- Content is **AI-assisted research and analysis** prepared for ZAFTYS readers.
- It is **not** a named individual author byline.
- It does **not** claim CA, legal, engineering, or independent third-party expert review.
- Fabricated human credentials and fabricated “last reviewed by [person]” dates are forbidden.

## Claim rules

- Prefer primary statutes, OEM product pages/brochures, and official government docs.
- Label workshop maths, teaching corridors, and synthetic latency examples as such.
- OEM specification tables: publish manufacturer-stated values only; otherwise write **Not stated in the cited source**.
- ULIP: describe the official pathway and readiness risks; publish measured p50/p95 only from a dated authorised test report (none fabricated).
- Freight-cost scenarios (including ₹54,000 / ₹62,000 / ₹55,800): hypothetical arithmetic unless an audited trip file exists.
