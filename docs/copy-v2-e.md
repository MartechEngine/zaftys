# How TranZfort Works

## Step One

Customer Requirement

The customer shares shipment details, timelines, cargo information, and transport requirements with ZAFTYS.

---

## Step Two

Operational Planning

The operations team evaluates fleet availability, route requirements, vehicle suitability, and delivery priorities.

---

## Step Three

Capacity Assessment

If company operated fleet capacity is sufficient, transport is assigned internally.

If additional vehicles are required, the TranZfort network becomes part of the operational plan.

---

## Step Four

Verified Partner Allocation

Suitable verified transport partners are identified based on operational requirements, location, vehicle availability, and service standards.

---

## Step Five

Centralized Coordination

Customers continue communicating with ZAFTYS.

Dispatch, documentation, and operational monitoring remain centrally coordinated.

---

## Step Six

Shipment Visibility

Progress is monitored through ZAFTYS TSM, providing consistent operational visibility throughout the shipment lifecycle.

---

## Step Seven

Delivery

Freight is delivered according to customer requirements with standardized documentation and communication.
