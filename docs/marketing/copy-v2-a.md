Never describe only the feature.

Explain the outcome.

Poor Example

Live GPS Tracking.

Better Example

Monitor every active shipment through one centralized dashboard with live vehicle locations and estimated arrival updates.

Poor Example

Digital Documentation.

Better Example

Store proof of delivery, invoices, transport documents, and shipment records securely so teams spend less time searching and more time operating.

Every feature should answer one question.

Why does this matter?

---

# Benefit Writing Framework

Benefits should describe business improvement.

Not software capability.

Instead of

Route Optimization

Write

Reduce unnecessary travel by selecting more efficient routes based on operational priorities.

Instead of

Fleet Analytics

Write

Understand fleet utilization, operational performance, and transport costs through centralized reporting.

Business outcomes always matter more than software terminology.
