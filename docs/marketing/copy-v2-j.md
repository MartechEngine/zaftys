# Frequently Asked Questions

## Heading

Sales And Consultation FAQs

---

### Is there any cost for the first consultation?

The initial consultation is focused on understanding your business requirements and discussing possible logistics solutions.

It provides an opportunity for both teams to determine whether ZAFTYS is the right long-term partner.

---

### Can demonstrations be customized?

Yes.

Product demonstrations are tailored according to your industry, business processes, fleet size, operational priorities, and existing technology environment.

---

### How long does implementation take?

Implementation timelines depend on project scope, operational complexity, required integrations, and customer readiness.

A detailed implementation plan is prepared before the project begins.

---

### Do you support businesses operating across multiple locations?

Yes.

ZAFTYS supports organizations managing logistics across multiple plants, warehouses, project sites, regional offices, and distribution centers.

Solutions are designed around centralized operational visibility and standardized workflows.

---

### Can ZAFTYS integrate with existing systems?

Yes.

Our long-term platform strategy includes integration with ERP systems, accounting software, warehouse management systems, GPS providers, and other enterprise applications where appropriate.

---

### What industries do you primarily support?

Our primary focus includes manufacturing, mining, cement, steel, engineering, construction, infrastructure, energy, industrial distribution, and project logistics.

---

### Can we start with one service and expand later?

Absolutely.

Many customers begin with transportation services or ZAFTYS TSM and gradually expand into dedicated fleet operations, verified transport capacity, AI-assisted workflows, or broader supply chain initiatives as their business grows.
