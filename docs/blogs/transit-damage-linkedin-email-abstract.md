## LinkedIn (short)

Most transit damage claims in Indian industrial freight fail on paperwork, not on the accident itself.

Clean lorry receipt. Blank declared value. Truck-stand broker. Late surveyor notice. That stack is how a multi-lakh machine collapses to a Rule 12 “ten times freight” recovery — and how ITC-A still repudiates.

We published a practical guide for plant logistics, procurement, and finance:

- Carriage by Road Act 2007 + Rule 12 declared-value path  
- Clean vs qualified LR (bilty) at the dock  
- Weighbridge shortage tolerance as a contract formula  
- 24-hour surveyor SOP + one-page checklist
