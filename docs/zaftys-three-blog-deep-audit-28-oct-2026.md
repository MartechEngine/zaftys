### Rail demurrage

- Exact claim that was on the page: the April 2024 compendium was the live step table, Corrigendum 56 had removed automatic penal demurrage, and six times the base was only a notified congestion rate. The 13 March 2026 letter had not been read.
- Official source consulted: [Corrigendum No. 61, 13 March 2026](https://rb.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/directorate/traffic_comm/2026/Review%20of%20Demurrage%20Penal%20Demurrage_001.pdf). The PDF was opened.
- What it establishes: from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 the base is ₹150 per eight-wheeled wagon per hour or part. The bands are base up to 6 hours, plus 10 percent beyond 6 up to 12, plus 25 percent beyond 12 up to 24, plus 50 percent beyond 24 up to 48, twice beyond 48 up to 72, three times beyond 72 up to 96, and six times beyond 96 hours. That last band is named the penal demurrage rate. The letter refers to Corrigendum 56 as valid only up to 31 March 2026. Waiver continues under the existing schedule of powers, and it reiterates paragraph 2.11 of Corrigendum 60. The 5 March 2026 letter is that waiver procedure. It is not this rate table.
- What it does not establish: a new free-time table, a new wharfage table, or the bill for any particular rake. 58 × ₹150 = ₹8,700 remains unit arithmetic for one hour in the first band.
- Final status: corrected. The rate table, the bars, the FAQs, and the demurrage board now follow Corrigendum 61. Free time and wharfage stay labelled as the April 2024 compendium, which this circular does not replace.

### Errors found and corrected in this pass

1. **National-highway permission fee was denied.** The ODC article said the bands of ₹1,200, ₹2,400, ₹3,600, and ₹4,800 per 50 km were a Madhya Pradesh charge and were not the national-highway portal fee. Paragraph 13 of MoRTH circular RW/NH-35072/01/2010-S&R(B) dated 27 October 2025 sets those exact bands for the national-highway journey, by HT band, when gross combination weight including the puller stays inside the permitted limit. The Madhya Pradesh Road Development Corporation order of 14 July 2025 uses the same bands on MPRDC roads. The article now states both fees and says neither is a trailer hire rate.
2. **Puller weight was stated as a fixed 25 tonnes.** The same circular's chart treats the puller as a 3-axle ballast tractor of up to 25 tonnes. The article now says "up to 25 tonnes with ballast." The HT gross-weight table remains without the puller.
3. **A six-month tanker examination was attributed to SMPV Rule 44(2).** The PESO-hosted Gazette text of the SMPV (Unfired) Rules, 2016, Rule 44, is the licensee's duty to keep the vehicle road-worthy. It has no sub-rule (2) and no six-month interval. That citation has been removed. Rules 18 and 19 were read in the same PDF and were left in place.
